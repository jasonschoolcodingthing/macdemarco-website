Mac DeMarco

Canadian Singer-Songwriter & Multi-Instrumentalist

Mac DeMarco is a Canadian singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist known for his laid-back, psychedelic pop sound that blends elements of indie rock, jazz, and vintage recording techniques. Born in Duncan, British Columbia in 1990, he moved to Edmonton to study music before relocating to Montreal where he began his recording career.

His music often features themes of love, nostalgia, and youthful rebellion, delivered with a distinctive lo-fi aesthetic and a voice that ranges from crooning ballads to playful falsetto. DeMarco's DIY approach to music production and his signature Fender Jaguar guitar have become hallmarks of his sound, influencing a generation of bedroom producers and indie musicians.
